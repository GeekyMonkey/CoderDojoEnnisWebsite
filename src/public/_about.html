About CoderDojo Ennis

CoderDojo Ennis is a free coding club for kids aged 7 and up. You can make things, learn new skills, and meet other coders.

What we do

CoderDojo clubs run around the world. Our Ennis club started in March 2012. We make games, websites, animations, and electronics projects.

If you’re new to coding, Scratch is a good place to start. Mentors help you learn how to use it and try other tools when you’re ready.

Working in teams

You can work with other kids on a project. Some people code, some draw, and some come up with ideas. You can try different jobs.

At show-and-tell, you can show the group what you’ve made. You don’t have to, and your project doesn’t have to be finished.

Our one rule

Above all: be cool.

Be kind, listen to each other, and help out when you can.
