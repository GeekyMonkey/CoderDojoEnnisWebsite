Join the club

You can join from age 7. You need basic reading and computer skills, but you don’t need to know how to code.

How to join

  1. Fill in the waiting-list form

    Ask a parent to help you fill in the form. We’ll contact you when there’s a place.

    Join the waiting list ↗

  2. Check your email

    When there’s a place, we’ll email you a start date and explain how to sign up.

  3. Bring your laptop

    Bring a laptop if you have one. If you need to use a club computer, ask us before you come.

Good to know

  • Sessions are free.
  • No coding experience needed.
  • Parents of children aged 12 and under must stay.
  • Parents are welcome to learn too.

Wait for your invitation before coming to your first session.

Help at the club

Adults can help with coding, art, paperwork, or looking after the group. You don’t need to be a programmer.

Email us to tell us how you’d like to help. Everyone who runs the club is a volunteer.
