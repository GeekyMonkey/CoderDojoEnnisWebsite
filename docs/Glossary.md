# CoderDojo Ennis

This glossary defines the language used for people, learning, and activities at CoderDojo Ennis.

## People and groups

**User**:
An individual in the CoderDojo Ennis community who may be a Coder, Parent, or Mentor, and may hold more than one of these roles.
_Avoid_: Member, Adult

**Coder**:
A User who takes part in dojo activities and works toward learning goals, Badges, and Belts.
_Avoid_: Ninja

**Parent**:
A User who is a guardian linked to one or more Coders; the term does not prescribe a specific legal relationship.
_Avoid_: Guardian

**Mentor**:
A User who supports Coders in their learning and dojo activities. This includes people giving coding advice and general club administration tasks.

**Team**:
A group of Coders with a shared identity and roster. A Coder belongs to at most one Team at a time.

## Learning and achievements

**Goal**:
An outcome a Coder is working toward, including short-term and long-term learning goals and selected badge goals.

**Badge goal**:
A goal to earn a selected Badge.

**Badge**:
A recognition a Coder can earn by demonstrating an achievement in a subject area.

**Badge category**:
A subject-area grouping for related Badges.

**Mentor skill**:
A subject area in which a Mentor can support Coders, associated with a Badge category.

**Belt**:
An ordered recognition in a Coder’s progression. Belts are awarded in a specific order, so the latest awarded Belt is the highest achieved.

**Achievement application**:
A Coder’s request to earn a Badge or Belt, supported by evidence for a Mentor to review.

**Achievement decision**:
A Mentor’s approval or rejection of an Achievement application, with any feedback provided as part of the decision.

## Sessions and attendance

**Session**:
A calendar entry for a scheduled dojo activity, which may include its topic, hosts, and joining details.

**Attendance**:
A record that a User was present on a particular dojo date. Joining a Session can record attendance; portal login alone does not.

**Portal login**:
Authentication into the online portal. A portal login does not by itself record attendance.

## Registration and account state

**Term registration**:
A Coder’s registration status for the current dojo term.

**Archived account**:
A User account retained with its history and relationships but no longer available for ordinary sign-in or use.
